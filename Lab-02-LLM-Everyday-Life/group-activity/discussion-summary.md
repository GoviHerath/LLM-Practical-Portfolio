# Discussion Summary & Reflection

## How LLMs Were Used
During the product development process for **UniSync AI**, an LLM was used as an interactive brainstorming partner:
* **Problem Breakdown:** Identified the core pain points of busy university students (overlapping deadlines, poor time blocking, and LMS alert fatigue).
* **Feature Ideation:** Generated and refined practical features like automated deadline extraction and one-click rescheduling.
* **User Flow:** Helped structure a clean, low-friction mobile dashboard and conversational UI.

## Key Observations
* LLMs are highly effective at rapidly generating diverse product concepts and refining user personas.
* Clear, structured prompts (specifying the target audience and constraints) produced much more realistic and actionable features than generic prompts.
* Human judgment was still necessary to filter out overly complex features and keep the product focused on student usability.

## Learning Outcomes
Learned how to leverage prompt engineering and LLMs effectively as a creative partner for real-world problem-solving and product conceptualization.
